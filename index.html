How it works

Four easy steps.

From camera roll to Lock Screen in about a minute. No tracing, no green screen, no account.

  1. 1

    Photo

    Choose a photo or take a new one. WallyPaper finds your kid or pet, lifts them off the background and frames them with their face in view.

  2. 2

    Style

    Tap a template. Stylize your photo, pick a holiday theme, or build your own look from solid backdrops and gradients.

  3. 3

    Name

    Add a name or nickname. Pick a font and style, and place it wherever it looks best.

  4. 4

    Set

    Save to Photos, then choose Share › Use as Wallpaper. WallyPaper walks you through it, and iOS adds the depth effect automatically when it fits.

Privacy

No account. No ads. No tracking.

WallyPaper processes your photos entirely on your iPhone and never sends them anywhere. We run no servers, so there is nothing to collect.

Photos stay on your iPhone

Your kid or pet is lifted from the photo by Apple's Vision framework on your device. WallyPaper uses the system photo picker, so it only ever sees the photo you choose.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. The app itself makes no network connections.

Apple handles purchases

Pro is sold through the App Store with StoreKit. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account information.
