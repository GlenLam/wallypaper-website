3. WallyPaper Pro

WallyPaper is free to use. WallyPaper Pro is a single upgrade, offered three ways through the App Store:

  • WallyPaper Pro Monthly: an auto-renewable subscription, $2.99 per month.
  • WallyPaper Pro Yearly: an auto-renewable subscription, $19.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • WallyPaper Pro Lifetime: a one-time purchase, $29.99.

Prices are in US dollars; the price in your local currency is shown in the App Store before you buy. Pro removes the WallyPaper watermark from exports and unlocks every gradient, mesh and holiday theme background, custom colors and custom two-color gradients, every font design and name style, custom name colors, and the Pro templates and art styles. What Pro includes may grow over time.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone; cancelling stops future renewals, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.
