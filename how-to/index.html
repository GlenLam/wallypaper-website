Make a Lock Screen wallpaper from a photo of your kid or pet.

Real photos, every step, nothing skipped. Each walkthrough starts with an ordinary snapshot and ends on an iPhone Lock Screen with a name on it, made in WallyPaper in about a minute.

The same four steps, every time

Photo. Style. Name. Set.

Whatever you start with, the walkthroughs all follow the app's four steps.

1. Photo

Choose a photo, take a new one, or share one to WallyPaper from the Photos app. WallyPaper finds your kid or pet, lifts them off the background on your iPhone and frames their face in view.

2. Style

Tap a template, pick a trading card or a holiday theme, or build your own look from solid backdrops, gradients and mesh gradients.

3. Name

Add a name or nickname. Six styles, four effects, any size, any position, even behind your subject.

4. Set

Save to Photos, then choose Share › Use as Wallpaper. WallyPaper walks you through it, and iOS adds the depth effect when it fits.
